# Lab Tools

Browser-based tools for cardiac and pulmonary MRI analysis, developed and run in
the Sun Lab. They are used by lab members and collaborators for image
segmentation, motion tracking and quantitative analysis.

## What the tools do

**Cardiac motion and strain**
Correction of automatic segmentations on tagged and cine MRI, with motion-guided
propagation across the cardiac cycle, producing regional strain and standard
volumetric measures (EDV, ESV, SV, EF, LV mass).

**Tissue characterisation**
T1 mapping and extracellular volume (ECV) analysis from native and post-contrast
MOLLI maps, with contour editing for longitudinal studies.

**Vascular function**
Aortic area, distensibility and pulse-wave velocity from 4D-flow and high-resolution
MRA, with user-supervised measurement rather than fully automatic segmentation.

**Pulmonary imaging**
Ventilation and perfusion mapping from free-breathing acquisitions, with manual
region correction.

**Myocardial perfusion**
Motion correction and quantitative myocardial blood flow, including segmental and
coronary-territory analysis.

**Neuroimaging**
Brain masking and atlas-based analysis for large-animal studies.

## For lab members

Each person has their own account and their own storage. Work you save is kept
under your name and cannot be overwritten by anyone else, and you can download a
copy of your results at any time.

Access is granted per tool, so you will see the tools relevant to your project.

## Notes

These tools run on lab hardware and operate on lab data. They are provided for
research use within the lab and its collaborations. They are not medical devices
and are not intended for clinical diagnosis.
